Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the game Gloopy Drop (Android, package com.kirilgd.gloopydrop) uses and why.

Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or receive any personal information. The game has no accounts, no registration and no servers of its own. Your progress (score, coins, levels, purchased skins and settings) is saved only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads between games. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • the device advertising ID;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • interactions with ads and diagnostic data (e.g. crashes of the ad component).

This data is handled by Google under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy settings inside the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Sharing your score

When you tap “Share”, the game creates a picture with your score on your device and opens the standard Android share menu. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app to share it with.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: doneckiril28@gmail.com
